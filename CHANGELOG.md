# Changelog

What changed in each Mica Slicer release, newest first. Each version's section is written once
and used everywhere: the app shows it after an update (Help › What's new…), and
`scripts/release.sh` publishes it as the GitHub release notes and in the update manifest.

A section starts with `## <version>`. Inside it: `###` headings, `-` bullets, `**bold**` and
`[links](https://…)`.

## 0.0.1

The first tester build. Mica is alpha (pre-release) software: check the preview before
printing and stay near your printer for the first layers.

### What you can do

- Slice STL and 3MF files, including PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer projects, with the same rules
  as **PrusaSlicer 2.9.6** and **OrcaSlicer**.
- Tested printers: Prusa CORE One, XL, MK3.5 and MINI, Snapmaker U1 and RatRig V-Core 3.2.
- Bring your printer, print and filament profiles over from PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer.
- Send prints to PrusaLink, Prusa Connect and Moonraker printers.
- Get new versions in the app: Check for Updates… looks for them.

Found a problem? [Report it on GitHub](https://github.com/steenfeldt/mica-slicer/issues).
